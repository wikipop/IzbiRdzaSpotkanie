# Asset provenance

The frontend contains no third-party illustrations, character art, logos, or bundled fonts.
Visual texture and decorative shapes are generated with project CSS.

The original generated Garnek illustrations are stored at
`public/assets/garnek/garnek.png` and `public/assets/garnek/garnek-sad.png`. Their creation method,
prompt summary, post-processing, and UI alt-text policy are recorded in
`public/assets/garnek/PROVENANCE.md`.

Do not add Doki Doki Literature Club assets or derivatives.
